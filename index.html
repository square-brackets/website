About

A decade of JavaScript, most of it on one large SaaS product rather than a parade of short projects — and now the team that builds it.

I still care most about the parts of the work that don't demo well: accessibility, the shape of a codebase two years after the people who wrote it have moved on, and pages that still load on a bad connection. Managing hasn't changed that — it just changed who I'm arguing for. Away from a screen I'm usually outdoors, on foot or on a bike, or cooking something that takes longer than it should.
